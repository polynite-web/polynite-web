Reduce the polygon count of a model

Polygon reduction is coming to Polynite. Today the optimizer does the part that usually matters more: it compresses the geometry and shrinks the textures, in your browser, with the before and after side by side.

Open the optimizerOpen a model

What works today

Open a GLB and Polynite tells you what it weighs and why: how many triangles, how many are drawn at the current distance, and the resolution of every texture next to its weight. Vertex attributes are quantized, the index buffer is reordered, the geometry is Meshopt compressed on export, and the textures are rebuilt at the quality you choose. The result is a standard GLB that opens in Blender, Unity, Godot, three.js and any glTF viewer.

Is the weight really in the triangles?

Usually not. A scan or a CAD export can arrive with millions of triangles, and that is real; but the file that is heavy on a web page is almost always heavy because of an 8K colour map on a prop that covers two hundred pixels. Polynite shows the split between geometry and textures, so you fight the right one first.

What is coming

Edge collapse driven by error, so the edges that carry the shape stay and the ones that do not go. Boundaries and UV seams treated as constraints, so the texture still lines up after a heavy reduction. The target as a proportion of the original, so the same setting behaves the same way on any model. Several steps from one source, picked by screen size, so the triangle count follows the camera. And the split line to drag across the model, because a number in a dialog cannot tell you that the ear of a character disappeared at sixty percent.

Questions people ask

Can Polynite reduce polygons today?

Not yet. Polygon reduction is on the road, and this page will say so the day it lands. What works today is the optimizer: geometry quantized and Meshopt compressed, textures shrunk, the before and after side by side.

What can I do about a heavy file right now?

Open it in the optimizer. In almost every real GLB the weight is in the textures, not the triangles: an 8K colour map on a small prop. Polynite shows the split between geometry and textures, so you know which one you are fighting.

What will the reduction look like?

Edges collapsed by error, boundaries and UV seams kept as constraints so the texture still lines up, a target given as a proportion of the original, several steps picked by screen size, and a split line to drag across the model to see exactly what went.

Where do I start?

With the optimizer, in your browser, with nothing uploaded: open a model, see what it weighs, export the version you can see.
