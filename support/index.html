A connection problem should not mean starting over.

Contact contact@majify.tech at Majify Technologies Inc..

Include browser/version, device, attempted action and visible error. Do not send OAuth tokens, room keys, private model files or signed video URLs unless specifically requested through an appropriate support channel.

The viewer disconnected

Bring the original view to the foreground and use Resume. A valid checkpoint restores the same scene. Do not open a replacement as a retry.

A filename, but no video link

Open Gallery → Videos and download locally. Ask for video_links after cloud transfer succeeds. A failed transfer preserves the local movie; do not record again just to retry upload.

A command failed

Correct rejected fields in the same scene. Inspect unknown outcomes before retrying to avoid duplicate effects.

Firefox stopped rendering

An unresolved long-session memory issue is under investigation. Save what remains available and report the error. Current browser validation focuses on desktop Chromium.

Data access or deletion

Contact us above and identify the relevant account or data without sending passwords/tokens. We may need to verify the request. MCP checkpoint/movie retention is seven days; other storage differs.
