01 / COMPOSE

How the recipe works

  1. Export a unique roof port from the provider, including its nested transforms.
  2. Attach the gallery to the doorway and bind the named roof context.
  3. Join only the consumer, fit its walls, inspect the seam and test changes before saving.

The engine supplies operators. The recipe chooses their arrangement, materials and behaviour. Change the composition to invent a different structure.

02 / ASK

Start a conversation

In Polynite discover roof, wall and context, then check commands.recipe_roof_ports. Let a provider roof export a named port, inspect roof_ports and attach an independent gallery at its authored doorway. Bind context:{name:hostroof,instance:hall,roof:main} and use roof join:@hostroof. Keep the host passage opening, gallery profiles, materials and repeated details in editable recipes. Estimate; change source proportions and pose; verify the dependent roof and walls. Preserve stable IDs and overrides, enable soft floor reflections, save and reopen the complete document.

Example and versioned recipe: https://explore.polynite.io/docs/recipes/linked-roof-workshop/

Paste into your connected assistant. A prompt invites a new interpretation; the scene and JSON preserve this exact reference.

03 / VERIFY

An example you can test.

These expectations are also consumed by the regression runner. Check geometry and behaviour, then look at the result.

  • roof_link · gallery · observed at 0 / 3 / 6 seconds

Directed, explicitly declared roof ports. The consumer yields to the provider and fits its walls to the shared envelope. The provider keeps its authored geometry and passage opening. No reciprocal dependency cycles, implicit host doorway, global wall welding or general solid Boolean. Geometry overrides on an exported roof must be removed or its port disabled first; material overrides remain supported.

For assistants & developers

Requires commands.recipe_program, commands.recipe_assemblies, commands.recipe_instances, commands.recipe_junctions, commands.recipe_roof_ports, commands.recipe_context, commands.surface_prisms from the connected engine. Discover operators before composing, estimate before applying, and preserve user edits.
