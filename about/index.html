What it does today

Create

Castles, villages and forests in one tap. Shapes from a sentence. A model from a picture. Export a GLB.

View

GLB first, OBJ and glTF too. Debug views for normals, depth, UVs, clusters, level of detail.

Optimize

Textures resampled, geometry compressed, before and after side by side.

Share

One link, live, with a Remix button. Publish it and it gets its own page.

Keep

A library in your own GitHub account, so what you publish follows you.

Simplify (coming)

Polygon reduction that keeps the silhouette and the UV seams.

Where it is going

The MCP door is under construction: any MCP client will build in the scene and read it back. Animation, films of a scene and heavier creations are next.

Your files

They stay on your machine. Sign in only to publish, into a repository you own.

Who makes it

A small independent team. Several components are on GitHub.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Creating, viewing, optimizing, sharing and exporting are free, with no account. AI generation uses your own Pollinations credits.

Who is it for?

Anyone with a browser and an idea, and anyone who needs to check or shrink a GLB in ten seconds.

Where is it going?

MCP connectivity, animation, films of a scene, heavier creations.
