An Endless Flight

A ship skims an endless world that changes as it flies: open sea under red cliffs, plains, forests and mountains, mirrored in the water, all generated on your device from a seed and driven through MCP.

Infinite worldMCPProceduralWaterShipEditable
Open & test ↗Get the scene
Make your own version
Create an endless flight in Polynite through MCP. Turn the infinite world on with scene.apply {op:'infinite', on:true, seed:7, follow:3}, hide the flat floor and keep a warm sky. Then shape it by name with the parameters listed in describe.infinite.params - oceans, rocky, water_reflect, boost, sparks - and add {op:'lens', on:true} for the sun.
