Make your own version
Create an infinite world in Polynite through MCP. Turn it on with scene.apply {op:'infinite', on:true, seed:7, follow:3}, then give it a ship recipe: {op:'infinite', ship_recipe:<JSON>} using the operators listed in polynite_capabilities.infinite_ship (loft, bore, planform, canopy, mount, neon; parts with hinges). Start from the linked scene's recipe and change its inputs with {op:'infinite', ship_params:{span:1.3, sweep:0.3, variety:1, seed:12}}.
