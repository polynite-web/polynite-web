Moonlit

Cozy Autumn Night

Polynite's complete demo: a stone cottage under the moon, volumetric light pouring from each window, a white garden lantern, a synthesised guitar score and a logo that dances to its notes. A perfect 15-second loop, generated on your device.

Open & test ↗Get the scene
Make your own version
Create a cozy stone cottage in Polynite on a round wooden plinth: autumn maples, a full moon and ground mist. Give each window a warm spot light with volume above 0 and a portal, so light pours through the air as they switch on. Add a garden lantern whose glass glows with its own light. Write a guitar score in initial.music_notes, played by synthesised voices with no audio file, and make the logo dance to it with {op:'logo_fx', on:true, dance:1, beat:1}. Finish with a seamless loop. Use the linked scene as a starting point.
