Create a Mint Link

  1. Prepare the scene, lighting and framing you want to share.
  2. Open Share. Take or select the scene card required by the sharing flow.
  3. Review the available content, visibility and remix/download settings.
  4. Select Mint Link and copy the resulting URL.
  5. Open the link in another tab to check the experience before sending it.

Minting can upload scene data, a card or an asset. An imported model and a procedural-only scene can use different storage paths. See Privacy.

View, then make it your own

The recipient can inspect the shared experience in a supported browser. Remix or download availability depends on the sharing options and the asset's license. A link does not automatically grant every permission.

Explore creations published by their authors in Discover. Follow the license on each creation's page.

Access and lifetime

A Mint Link is not a permanent backup. Anonymous uploaded shares are configured to expire after 72 hours; account-backed storage and publication have their own rules. Review the information shown when creating your link and keep your source files.

Share URLs can grant access to the people who hold them. Review visibility before publishing publicly, and use the available withdrawal controls if you want to remove access.
