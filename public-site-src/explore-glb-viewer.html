Opening a local model does not itself publish that model. Sharing, checkpoint restoration, cloud storage and connected AI workflows may upload data; see Privacy.

Browser and file compatibility

Use a WebGPU-capable browser; desktop Chromium is the exercised path. Support depends on the model's extensions, texture formats and available memory. Check complex or compressed assets in the target application before relying on an export.
