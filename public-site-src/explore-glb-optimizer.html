Geometry and texture budgets

The Optimize panel reports how the model's data is distributed. Texture resolution, vertex data, indices and animation can contribute differently from one file to another.

Available quality controls

Use the quality and compression settings available for the loaded model. Compare the visible result and reported size before exporting; a particular percentage saving is not guaranteed.

Check the destination

An exported GLB may use geometry compression or other extensions. Your destination viewer or engine must support those extensions; a valid GLB is not a guarantee of compatibility everywhere.

Local work, explicit sharing

Local inspection and optimization are separate from publication or connected services. Only share or upload a model through a workflow you intend to use.
