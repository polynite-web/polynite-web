Count, compress, simplify

Polygon count describes geometry complexity. Compression reduces encoded size; texture resampling changes image resolution. Simplification changes geometry and can affect silhouette, seams or animation.

Use the controls available for your model

Start with the Optimize panel's breakdown. Compare available quality settings and inspect the result. The applicable controls depend on the current build and asset; this page does not promise arbitrary retopology or lossless simplification.

Validate what matters

Look at close-up details, silhouettes and animated poses, then test the export in its destination application. File size alone does not establish rendering quality or compatibility.
