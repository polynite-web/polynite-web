Three ways to create

Procedural, in one tap. A castle with its walls, towers, keep and village; a tower; a village around its well; a forest of round trees, pines and rocks. Every tap makes a different one: the walls are stone or brick, the roofs red, slate or green, the village denser or sparser. It costs nothing and needs no account. It is also the fastest way to see what the materials and the light do before you write a word.

Shapes from a sentence. Describe what you want - a lighthouse on rocks, a table with four chairs, a small harbour - and an AI model builds it out of primitives: cubes, spheres, cylinders, cones, capsules and planes, each with a colour and a material. Polynite checks every action the model proposes and refuses what would not stand, so the model corrects itself turn after turn. It works through your Pollinations account.

A 3D model from text or a picture. Trellis, through the same account, turns a description or a photograph into a real mesh with textures. The form says what it costs in pollen before you press. The result opens in the viewer like any GLB, and can be shrunk, shared and exported from there.

Materials and light

Every object can wear a material with real relief: stone, brick, wood, tile, leaf, grass, cobble, marble, metal or sand. They are generated in the page - no texture is downloaded - and the sun, the lamps and the mirror floor read them the way they read a file's own maps. Everything you create is in metres, so a two metre wall is a two metre wall.

Edit with your hands

Tap an object and two handles appear: drag the centre to move it on the ground, drag the edge to turn and resize it. Every gesture is one step of undo. A loaded GLB is an object like the others - you can put a castle beside it, or stand it on a table you just made.

Export a GLB, free

Anything you create can be written as a standard .glb - one mesh with normals, UVs and vertex colours - and opened in Blender, Unity, Godot, three.js or any glTF viewer. Bake it first and the same file is loaded back through Polynite's cluster builder, so a creation of hundreds of thousands of triangles stays smooth on a phone.

Let an agent build

The same actions are open to programs. Any MCP client can create, move and describe objects in your scene through the Polynite MCP server.

Requirements

A browser with WebGPU: a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, over HTTPS. The procedural creations need nothing else. The AI ways need a free Pollinations account, and the 3D model costs pollen.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. Creating procedurally, viewing, editing by hand, sharing and exporting a GLB are free, with no account. Generating shapes or a full model with AI goes through your own Pollinations account and costs its credits.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Polynite runs in a browser with WebGPU: a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or a phone.

Can I download what I make?

Yes. Anything you create exports as a standard .glb that opens in Blender, Unity, Godot, three.js and any glTF viewer.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere, unless you decide. A model you open is read by the page and stays on your machine. Sharing or publishing is a separate, explicit step, into a link or a repository you own.
