Questions people ask

Where do the megabytes go?

Almost always into textures. Polynite shows the split between geometry and textures, so you fight the right one.

Will the result still open everywhere?

Yes. A standard GLB with Meshopt compression, read by any glTF engine.

Can I see what I lose?

Yes, before you export: numbers side by side and a split view of the same frame.
