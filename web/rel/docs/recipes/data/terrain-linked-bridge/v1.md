# A bridge between living shores

An editable arched bridge follows two construction anchors and clears sampled terrain. Deck, rails and feet share one profile; the same operators can compose a different crossing.

## Prompt

In Polynite, create an editable terrain-adaptive bridge. Discover commands.recipe_terrain and architecture operators context/spatial/let. Bind named left and right references to real construction anchors and ground to the enabled infinite terrain. Compose shared primitives, beams, expressions and stable anchors as editable JSON recipes. Sample the centre and both edges. Keep construction tops above ground plus thickness and clearance; preserve manual edits. Expose width, bend, arch and rail_height. Estimate, apply, inspect the connections, then verify the actual pixels. Keep the current user scene; invent a variation instead of adding a native generator for this style.

## Recipe composition

1. Bind existing terrain and construction anchors explicitly.
2. Evaluate one sampled profile at the centre and both edges; join segments and reuse its heights for rails and feet.
3. Keep the shape, materials and connection policy in editable recipe data; terrain/source edits revalidate declared dependants.

## Settings

Adjust > bridge · recipe controls: width, bend, arch, thickness, clearance, rail_height and segments. Adjust > shore_a/shore_b: lift, width and depth. The same controls live under Scene settings > Living architecture.

## Try a variation

- Press Play to compare the initial ground, a raised patch and its restored state.
- Under Adjust, change bridge width or bend. Change shore_b lift to raise the arrival and watch dependent connections follow.
- Use reusable landings to compose a longer route; keep sharp turns and obstacle avoidance explicit.

## Limits

Uses explicit local bindings and sampled near-field terrain, not arbitrary mesh raycasts, continuous collision or automatic pathfinding. Span 0.5..20m; 8..20 segments. max_grade limits end-to-end rise, not every local segment. Inspect steep relief and bends, increase samples or add intermediate landings. Invalid references, buried ends and invalid spans reject without changing the last valid scene.

Recipes are editable examples, not a closed list of shapes. Compose or invent alternatives using the connected engine's discovered operators. Geometry and motion execute locally; the server distributes recipe data.
