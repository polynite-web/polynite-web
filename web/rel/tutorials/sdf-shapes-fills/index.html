Creation prompt
Create a gallery of filled SDF shapes in Polynite over a small night studio.

- Reuse a studio on the standard floor (pedestal, glowing sphere, cube, cone) and read scene.shape help.
- Upsert one screen shape, style 'neon', size 0.42: a rounded box (size [36,24], r 5, angle 12, fill and stroke, color 2), a green ellipse (color [0.4,1,0.55,1]), a filled star, a pie sector (r 16, a0 90, a1 -150) and a filled heart as an SVG path with two cubic curves.
- Second row: a box with a circle cut out of it (op:'subtract'), two discs merged with op:'smooth' (k 6), a dashed circle (dash [4,3]), twelve ticks from one line with repeat {radial:12, c} and a curved arrow with a dot tail.
- Trace it in over 3.2 s, fade it out, and show it with a film step {op:'shape', at:0.4, dur:11.4, id:'fills'} under a slow camera orbit.
- Check status: cost.tiles and cost.vs_one_quad show how the shape was cut into quads that fit its ink.
Try 3 guided experiments
  1. Your own outline
    Any SVG path works: ask for {path:'M ... C ... Z', fill:true} - a logo, a leaf, a silhouette. Several sub-paths make holes.
  2. Melt them
    Raise the smooth union's k from 6 to 15: the two discs flow into one blob.
  3. Compare the cost
    Add tiles:'one' to the shape and read status.cost: one quad does the same picture with several times more work.

Requires the Polynite engine with SDF shapes (the scene.shape command). Shapes are saved inside the scene and replayed by its film; nothing is uploaded.
