Infinite worlds / Alpine

An Endless Flight

A ship skims an endless, deterministic mountain world: forests, lakes and a five-kilometre horizon, generated on your device from a seed and built entirely through MCP.

Infinite worldMCPProceduralShipEditable
Open & test ↗Get the scene
Make your own version
Create an endless mountain world in Polynite with a ship flying through it. Check scene.capabilities for the infinite operation, turn it on with scene.apply {op:'infinite', on:true, seed:7}, hide the flat floor, add a warm afternoon sky, lakes at 48 m and a forest cover of 0.6, let the ship fly on its terrain-following autopilot and orbit the camera around it with follow 3 and a loop_orbit (yaw 0 behind the ship). Expose speed, flight height, seed, lake level and forest cover in Scene settings. Use the linked scene as a starting point.

Make your own version
In Polynite, compose a detailed Japanese pagoda from nested assemblies. Define a bracket group, repeat it in a frontage, repeat the frontage around a storey, then repeat uniformly scaled storeys with taper. Use shared swept roof panels and ribs, procedural vermilion wood, dark tiles, warm paper and gold fittings. Keep storey count, width, taper, eave lift and detail switches editable.
