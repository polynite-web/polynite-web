D11: a guard drone with a mind of its own

One minute, 9:16. A small drone walks into a trap: three turrets rise out of the floor. Its shield pops, it crashes, you watch its Nod mind reboot, it comes back, wins, and sings. Recorded as the game plays.

Make your own version
Make a small game in Polynite through MCP whose rules film themselves. Lay out an arena with scene.apply (objects, lights, emitters), then give it a mind: scene.apply {op:'nod', graphs:[{id:'guard', states:['up','down'], vars:{life:10}, rules:[{id:'hit', on:'every:0.1', when:'life < 3', do:[{op:'voice.say', voice:'drone_voice', say:'Shields low!'}, {op:'camera.move', at:'drone', zoom:0.6, time:2}]}]}]}. Each rule is an event, a condition and actions (voice.say, voice.sing, camera.move, emitter.fire, vfx.set, film.end). Then play a film of it with scene.cinematic {command:'film_play', name:'Intro + Battle'} and record it with scene.cinematic {command:'record', format:'portrait'}. Use the linked scene as a starting point.
