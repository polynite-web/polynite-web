Sea, rock and mountains

Star Veyland: a world with no edge

Sea, rock and mountains are generated on your device from a seed and keep coming as you fly. The ship is a recipe of parts you can tune, and the whole scene is driven through MCP.

Open & test ↗Get the scene
Make your own version
Create an infinite world in Polynite through MCP. Turn it on with scene.apply {op:'infinite', on:true, seed:7, follow:3}, then give it a ship recipe: {op:'infinite', ship_recipe:<JSON>} using the operators listed in polynite_capabilities.infinite_ship (loft, bore, planform, canopy, mount, neon; parts with hinges). Start from the linked scene's recipe and change its inputs with {op:'infinite', ship_params:{span:1.3, sweep:0.3, variety:1, seed:12}}.

Sea, rock and mountains

An Endless Flight

A ship skims an endless world that changes as it flies: open sea under red cliffs, plains, forests and mountains, mirrored in the water, all generated on your device from a seed and driven through MCP.

Open & test ↗Get the scene
Make your own version
Create an endless flight in Polynite through MCP. Turn the infinite world on with scene.apply {op:'infinite', on:true, seed:7, follow:3}, hide the flat floor and keep a warm sky. Then shape it by name with the parameters listed in describe.infinite.params - oceans, rocky, water_reflect, boost, sparks - and add {op:'lens', on:true} for the sun. Use the linked scene as a starting point.
