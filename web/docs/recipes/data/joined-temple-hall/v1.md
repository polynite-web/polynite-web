## Prompt

In Polynite, discover architecture.operators for roof and wall and check commands.recipe_junctions. Compose an editable Japanese temple from a local joined roof and mitered wall chain. Cut the door and windows as real openings. Keep curvature, door width, materials and ornament choices in a module:assembly recipe; use shared prototypes for columns and repeated brackets. Estimate before applying. Change the roof rise and door width, inspect contacts and voids, save, reopen and resume by patching the same instance ID. Preserve the current scene.

## Recipe composition

1. Define named roof wings and their recipe-owned profiles.
2. Reference the roof from a wall chain; declare real door and window voids.
3. Compose shared columns, brackets and ornaments; estimate, inspect, capture, save and reopen.

## Limits

Bounded local joins: up to four roof wings, level wall chains and rectangular openings. Roof upper faces are trimmed at intersections; underside seams are not a general solid Boolean. Separate wall chains are not globally welded. Style, profiles, ornaments and placement remain editable recipe data.

Recipes are editable examples, not a closed list of shapes. Compose or invent alternatives using the connected engine's discovered operators. Geometry and motion execute locally; the server distributes recipe data.
