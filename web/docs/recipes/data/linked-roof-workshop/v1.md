Bind context:{name:hostroof,instance:hall,roof:main} and use roof join:@hostroof. Keep the host passage opening, gallery profiles, materials and repeated details in editable recipes. Estimate; change source proportions and pose; verify the dependent roof and walls. Preserve stable IDs and overrides, enable soft floor reflections, save and reopen the complete document.

## Recipe composition

1. Export a unique roof port from the provider, including its nested transforms.
2. Attach the gallery to the doorway and bind the named roof context.
3. Join only the consumer, fit its walls, inspect the seam and test changes before saving.

## Limits

Directed, explicitly declared roof ports. The consumer yields to the provider and fits its walls to the shared envelope. The provider keeps its authored geometry and passage opening. No reciprocal dependency cycles, implicit host doorway, global wall welding or general solid Boolean. Geometry overrides on an exported roof must be removed or its port disabled first; material overrides remain supported.

Recipes are editable examples, not a closed list of shapes. Compose or invent alternatives using the connected engine's discovered operators. Geometry and motion execute locally; the server distributes recipe data.
