About Polynite

A 3D viewer and creation environment in your browser, with MCP connectivity. Make a scene from a tap, a sentence or a picture, open any GLB, make it lighter, share it in one link, let the next person remix it, and let an agent build beside you.

Why a browser

Most people who need to check or shrink a GLB do not want to install anything, and the ones who already own a 3D suite do not want to open it to answer a question that takes ten seconds. A page that loads, reads the file locally and draws it removes that whole step. WebGPU is what makes it viable: the same cluster based renderer that would need a native build a few years ago now runs on a tab.

What it does today

Create

Procedural castles, towers, villages and forests in one tap; shapes from a sentence with an AI model; a 3D model from text or a picture. Move what you made by hand, export it as a GLB.

View

GLB first, and debug views for normals, depth, UVs, clusters and level of detail. OBJ and text glTF open too.

Optimize

Geometry quantization, Meshopt compression, texture resampling, and a side by side comparison before you export.

Simplify (coming)

Polygon reduction that keeps the silhouette and the UV seams, with several steps chosen by screen size. On the road; the optimizer already takes the textures down today.

Keep

An optional library backed by your own GitHub account, so a model you publish follows you from one machine to the next.

Where it is going

The MCP door is under construction: any MCP client will build in the scene, read it back and correct itself, in the same window you are looking at. Animation and rigging of what you create, films of a scene, and heavier creations that travel the same road as a big GLB, are next. This page will say so as they land.

Your files

Models you open are read by the page and stay on your machine. Signing in is optional and is never required to view or optimize anything. When you do sign in, Polynite stores what you publish in a repository you own and can take back at any time.

Who makes it

Polynite is built by a small independent team. The source of several components, including the connectors, is published on GitHub.

Questions people ask

Who is it for?

Anyone with a browser and an idea: people who want to make something in 3D without learning a suite, people who need to check or shrink a GLB in ten seconds, and people who want to send a model to someone as one link.

Is it free?

Creating, viewing, editing, optimizing, sharing and exporting are free, with no account. AI generation uses your own Pollinations account. A library of your published models lives in your own GitHub repository.

Where is it going?

An MCP server already lets an agent build in the scene. Animation and rigging of what you create, films of a scene, and heavier creations are next.
